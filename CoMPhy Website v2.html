CoMPhy Lab Computational Multiphase Physics

We study non-Newtonian free-surface flows and soft-matter singularities — bubbles, drops, sheets, and jets that break or bounce.

A small group at the intersection of applied mathematics, fluid mechanics and direct numerical simulation. We develop open-source code, train students, and publish with collaborators in Enschede, Lausanne, Paris, and Princeton.

01 · Research

Four themes, one question: how does soft matter break?

We look for the universal mechanisms that govern fast, often singular flows. Each theme has a hero figure, a two-sentence framing, and the three papers that introduce it best — not the fifteen we've written.

01 · Free-surface flows

Bubble bursting & Worthington jets

When a surface bubble bursts, the collapsing cavity launches a fast, thin jet. We map the transition from smooth jets to break-up across viscosity and elasticity.

2025 Bursting bubbles in yield-stress fluids PRL
2024 Role of viscosity in the collapse of a cavity JFM
2022 Jetting to no-jetting transition in elastic bubbles Soft Matter
02 · Singularities

Holes & Taylor–Culick retractions

A hole in a thin sheet is an interface that runs: rims accelerate, then break into droplets. We study the double-threshold rupture of draining films, with and without surfactant.

2026 Holes in sheets: double-threshold rupture PRL
2024 Surfactant-laden film retraction revisited JFM
2021 Universal scaling for rim instabilities PRE
03 · Impact & spreading

Drops, beads & splashes

Drop impact is the cleanest lab system for singular flow. We unify the rheology of liquid drops and soft elastic beads into a single impact-regime diagram.

2026 Impacting spheres: from liquid drops to elastic beads Soft Matter (cover)
2023 Elastoviscoplastic drop impact on solid surfaces JFM
2022 Splashing thresholds for Bingham drops PRF
Figure: Basilisk mesh · adaptive AMR
04 · Methods

Open-source DNS for complex fluids

We contribute to Basilisk — adaptive-mesh DNS for two-phase, non-Newtonian, and elastoviscoplastic flows. Every paper ships a reproducible simulation repository.

2024 A conservative VOF scheme for Bingham fluids JCP
2023 Adaptive AMR benchmarks for multiphase DNS IJNMF
2021 Regularised viscoplastic free-surface solvers JNNFM
